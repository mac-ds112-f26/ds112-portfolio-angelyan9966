My Experience

My experiences span media production, communication, journalism, education, community engagement, and research. I have worked with visual storytelling, digital media, and cross-cultural communication.

Media and Communication

Saint Paul Neighborhood Network

Communication Intern
Saint Paul, Minnesota | Jun 2026 – Dec 2026

Created digital promotional materials including posters, web banners, social media graphics, and multimedia content for nonprofit campaigns and community events.

Worked with staff and community partners to develop inclusive communication materials and strengthen community engagement.

Also supported video production, photography, visual storytelling, and live event production.

Xinhua News Agency

News Production Intern
Guangzhou, China | Jun 2026 – Aug 2026

Developed interview outlines, production plans, and shooting scripts for news coverage.

Edited news videos for digital platforms and created multimedia content introducing Chinese society, culture, and current affairs to international audiences.

Yew Chung Education Foundation

Content Creator
Hong Kong, China | Jul 2026 – Jan 2027

Developed creative ideas for video and social media content focused on student life, academic programs, and organizational storytelling.

Collaborated with the alumni team on alumni spotlights, videos, social media stories, events, and newsletters.

Yew Chung Education Management

Marketing Intern
Shanghai, China | May 2025 – Jul 2025

Supported school events, marketing projects, and external partnerships.

Contributed ideas for improving publicity and student resource use, and conducted research on the international education market.

Education & Community Engagement

International Student Program

Mentor Dean / Mentor
Macalester College | 2025 – 2026

Mentored incoming international students as they adjusted to college life and the U.S. cultural environment.

Later served as a Mentor Dean, helping train new mentors, organize meetings, and support the mentor team.

Film Analysis / Visual Culture

Preceptor / Teaching Assistant
Macalester College | Jan 2026 – May 2026

Supported class discussions, film screenings, office hours, and study sessions.

Helped students analyze visual media and film scenes and provided feedback on weekly coursework.

Research Experience

Stigma and Well-Being: An Interdisciplinary Perspective

Researcher
University College London | May 2023 – Jul 2023

Worked with qualitative and quantitative research methods while studying stigma, minority representation, and social perception.

Contributed to an interdisciplinary white paper and presented research during group meetings.

Published a supervised research paper examining campus anti-bullying and LGBTQ+ communities.

China Pacific Insurance Group

Marketing Intern
Aug 2023 – Sep 2023

Conducted industry research using both qualitative and quantitative methods.

Worked with survey distribution, industry reports, and group analysis to support marketing research and decision-making.
